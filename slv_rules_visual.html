R1 is the volume killer.

One percent of CMFs, twenty-one percent of orders. High-headcount ship-tos concentrate the volume — the 751 with fifty-plus contacts alone cover ten percent of every order booked.

R2 is the headcount killer.

Half of all ordering contacts placed fewer than three orders total in the year. They cannot pass R2 at any ship-to, ever. The rule shuts out 611,259 contacts.

R3 without distributor.

Once Action 1 carves out distributors — 20,870 CMFs — R3 shrinks to government, college, and hospital: roughly 26K CMFs and 613K orders, 3.5% of the universe.

R4 punches above its weight.

Only 43,487 contacts fail R4 — 3.1% of the universe — but they average 43 orders each versus the universe average of 14. Small headcount, heavy order density.
