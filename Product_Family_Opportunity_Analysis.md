# Product-Family Opportunities — Read & Synthesized into Families

*Method: 25 Claude readers read all ~12,265 opportunity descriptions, let product **families emerge** from the text, placed each in a Domain→Group→Family outline, and estimated how big each family would get in the real world. This report is organized **by those 5 size buckets**, family-first.*

**12,286 opportunities** read · **8,576 distinct families** synthesized.

## Size-bucket distribution (by opportunity)

| Bucket | Meaning | Opportunities | Share |
|---|---|--:|--:|
| **1** | Accessories / replacement parts | 1,250 | 10% |
| **2** | Genuinely new, small (<100) | 4,705 | 38% |
| **3** | Niche, real size×material depth (100–500) | 4,834 | 39% |
| **4** | Deep family / system (500–5,000) | 1,375 | 11% |
| **5** | Massive / universal commodity (>5,000) | 122 | 1% |

> Unlike a keyword pass (which buries ~90% in 'small'), reading the descriptions with world knowledge surfaces real depth: ~39% of opportunities are niche components whose world inventory runs 100–500 items, and ~11% are genuinely deep families or systems.

> **Caveat:** 25 independent readers means Group/Family names are not perfectly harmonized and a few bucket-4 calls are generous. The bucket split and Domain rollup are robust; treat individual family placements as a first synthesis to validate.
